## Our Mission

CodiceSolidale is a non-profit association that connects **developers, designers, makers, communicators and technology enthusiasts** with nonprofit organizations that need digital tools.

Our goal is to break down the technological barriers that prevent third-sector organizations from realizing their full potential. We do this by building open source software together — free, sustainable and community-owned.

---

## Who We Are

We are a diverse and open community. Among us you'll find:

- **Software developers** with experience in web, mobile and cloud technologies
- **UX/UI designers** who make interfaces accessible and intuitive
- **Communicators** who amplify NGO messages on social media and in the press
- **Humanitarian volunteers** with field experience in nonprofit organizations
- **Makers and enthusiasts** who want to put their skills at the service of communities
- **Anyone** with ideas, enthusiasm and a desire to contribute

There are no entry requirements. Contributions can be small or large, technical or non-technical.

---

## Our Values

### Openness
Everything we build is open source. Code, documentation and processes are public and accessible to anyone who wants to learn, contribute or fork.

### Inclusivity
We welcome contributions from anyone, regardless of technical experience, geographical origin or professional background. Newcomers are as welcome as experts.

### Measurable Impact
Before starting a project, we always ask: *who does it help? how many people does it involve? is it sustainable over time?* We work to build tools that last and have a real, verifiable impact.

### Respect and Collaboration
Our community is built on respectful communication, constructive feedback and mutual help. We have a code of conduct that all participants agree to.

---

## Our History

CodiceSolidale was founded in 2022 by a group of developers in Milan who, during the pandemic, experienced firsthand how the lack of adequate digital tools slowed down humanitarian organizations' work.

The first project was a simple coordination app for a local soup kitchen. Within months, the community grew organically: more developers joined, then designers, communicators, and eventually nonprofit organizations began proposing their own needs.

Today CodiceSolidale has completed over 45 projects across 18 countries, with an active community of over 250 volunteers.

---

## How We're Organized

We function as a distributed, self-managed community:

- **Project Leads** coordinate individual projects, organize meetings and manage milestones
- The **Board of Directors** (annually elected) handles governance, NGO agreements and fundraising
- **Mentors** support newcomers in navigating the community and the technologies we use

Every significant decision is discussed publicly on GitHub or in our community channels.
